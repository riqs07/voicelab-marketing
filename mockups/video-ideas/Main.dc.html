The pitch roller coaster

A little cart rides your actual pitch line. A flat take is a dull, level ride; a lively one climbs and drops with the riders' hands in the air. A statement that rises at the end sends the cart off a ramp: “That sounded like a question.”

Driven by The app's pitch line for each take (the track's shape), its phrase endings (the ramp) and its flat-phrase check (the yawning riders).
