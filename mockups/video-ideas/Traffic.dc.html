Words as traffic

Each word is a car, as long as it took to say. The rushed take is a traffic jam with “um” vans squeezing in; the calm take of the same news flows, with clear gaps where the pauses are.

Driven by Word timings (car lengths), the pause finder (the gaps) and the pause-word count (the vans).
Cost None: reuses the rushed and calm takes from the Pause Power video.

Sketch only: car lengths are stand-ins, not measured word times.
