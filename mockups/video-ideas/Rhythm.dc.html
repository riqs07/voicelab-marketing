Read aloud as a rhythm game

The passage falls down lanes like a music game. Each word flashes green the moment it's read; pause marks are rests you hold; a skipped word shatters. It ends on the score: how much of the speech came through.

Driven by Read aloud's live word tracking (which word is next, which were read) and its reading check (the skipped word, the score).
Cost One read-through of the passage in a stock voice (about a cent), timed with the app's tracker. Could also become the live view of the free Read aloud on the site.

Sketch only: timing is evenly spaced, not a real reading.
