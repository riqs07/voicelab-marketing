A voice that fills the room

The same line, said three times, and the walls grow with the measured loudness: a phone booth, a meeting room, a hall where the audience turns to listen. Then the “drop for effect” take: the room closes in and everyone leans forward.

Driven by The Volume drills' measurements: the level of each take and the size of each step (Three rooms, then Drop for effect).
Cost Four short takes in one voice, at three levels and one quiet (a few cents), measured with the app's level reader.
