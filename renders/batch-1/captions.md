## 2. Um rain
Video: `UmRain-story.mp4` · Cover: `covers/UmRain-cover.png`

```
12 pause words in 20 seconds.

Every “um”, “uh” and “like” drops into the jar the moment it's said. Then the same news, told calmly: two short pauses, an empty jar.

A pause sounds like you meant it. An “um” sounds like you're still looking for it.

Try it: tell someone about your week without a single “um”. Harder than it sounds.

## 5. One sentence. Five meanings. (Stress shift)
Video: `StressShift-story.mp4` · Cover: `covers/StressShift-cover.png`

```
“I didn't say she stole it.”

Move the stress and the meaning changes:
I → someone else said it
say → I only hinted it
she → someone else did
stole → she borrowed it
it → she stole something else

Where your voice lifts decides what people hear.

Which version have you heard in an argument?

## Notes
