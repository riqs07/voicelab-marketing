# VoiceLab video scripts, v1 (for approval)

Voices: **Narrator** = Caroline (warm, bright). **Speaker** = Nate (natural, podcast). One take per line.
Every video ends on the same end card: logo, "See how you sound.", "Try it free at [YOUR URL]".
Captions are on screen throughout. Formats: 9:16 and 4:5 (the demo also 16:9).

| Time | On screen | Narrator |
